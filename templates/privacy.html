Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects information necessary to provide our expense tracking service. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Expense Data: Transaction details including date, amount, category, description, and any attached notes.
  • Device Information: Information about the device you use to access Spendly, including IP address and browser type.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our application to help us improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our expense tracking service.
  • To help you manage and understand your personal finances.
  • To communicate with you about updates, changes to our service, and support.
  • To ensure the security and integrity of our platform.
  • To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely on our servers with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party services. However, we may use third-party service providers for infrastructure, analytics, and security purposes. These providers are contractually obligated to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data.

We do not use tracking pixels, cookies, or similar technologies to monitor your behavior outside of Spendly.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Export your expense data in a standard format.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@spendly.com.
